Most of that intent evaporates the moment the call ends, because nobody had time to write it down. Automotive Connect's AI stack — AIR, ACE, and AVA — captures it instead, turning every conversation into structured data and next steps automatically.

## How AI handles a call, start to finish

1. A call comes in to the dealership's RingEX number.
2. AIR answers, identifies the reason for the call, and either routes it to the right desk or books the appointment directly.
3. Once a rep is on the line, AVA surfaces relevant customer and vehicle context so the rep can pick up the conversation without asking the customer to repeat themselves.
4. ACE transcribes the call and extracts structured details — vehicle, intent, next steps — writing them into the DMS or CRM automatically.
5. AVA drafts the follow-up message based on the call, so the rep can review, personalize, and send it in seconds.
