## The phone gap is costing your dealership real money

Every unanswered call is a missed appointment. Every conversation that never makes it into the DMS is a lead your CRM doesn't know exists. Multiply that across every desk on your lot, every day, and the gap between "we handled the call" and "we captured the business" gets expensive fast.
