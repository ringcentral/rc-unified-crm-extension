!!! info "ACE requires a license to log AI artifacts"
    AI Assistant's live transcription and call summaries are available today. Logging those artifacts automatically into your matter records requires an [AI Conversation Expert (ACE)](https://www.ringcentral.com/ringsense.html) license.

## How AI handles a client call, start to finish

1. A client calls the firm's RingEX number.
2. Whoever picks up has AI Assistant running, generating a live transcript so no detail of the client's situation gets lost or misremembered.
3. When the call ends, AI Assistant produces a summary, key takeaways, and any action items or decisions made on the call.
4. With ACE enabled, those artifacts are logged automatically against the matching contact and matter — no manual data entry, no billable time lost to admin work.
5. The next person who picks up the file — attorney, paralegal, or billing — has the full, accurate record in front of them.
