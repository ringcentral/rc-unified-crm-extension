# App Connect for Legal Teams

Every client call is either time you bill or time you write off. When intake calls go unanswered, when a conversation never makes it into the matter file, or when an associate spends ten minutes typing up notes that AI could have generated in real time, your firm is paying for it twice — once in the missed business, and again in the hours nobody can invoice. App Connect brings RingCentral's calling, texting, and AI stack directly into the practice management software your firm already runs on, so every client interaction is captured, logged, and billable without extra data entry.

App Connect logs every call and text against the right contact and the right matter automatically, so nothing depends on someone remembering to write it down. Attorneys and paralegals get a complete, accurate record of every client conversation without touching a keyboard, and billing staff get clean, auditable time entries instead of chasing down what happened on a call three days ago.

## Feature highlights

* **Track calls as billable time** — every call is captured with duration and timestamp, ready to convert into a time entry instead of being reconstructed from memory.
* **Auto-track time reading and responding to SMS** — text conversations with clients are logged and timed just like calls, so billable text time doesn't disappear either.
* **Log and track faxes** *(Clio exclusive)* — fax activity is captured and logged against the matter alongside calls and texts.
* **Associate calls with medical providers** *(Clio exclusive)* — for personal injury and similar practice areas, calls can be linked directly to the medical provider contact involved in a case.
* **Associate calls with matters** — calls log against the specific matter they relate to, not just the contact, so time and activity roll up to the right case automatically.
