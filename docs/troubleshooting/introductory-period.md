# How long will the App Connect introductory period last?

There is no planned end date for the App Connect 2.0 introductory period at this time.

## What this means for you

During this period, logging AI-generated call transcripts, summaries, and other insights to your CRM is available at no additional cost, without requiring a separate RingCentral ACE license. See [What does App Connect cost?](app-connect-cost.md) for the full breakdown of what's free and what isn't.

## How we'll notify you of a change

If RingCentral decides to end the introductory period, or move this capability to a paid model, customers will be given ample advance notice so they can plan accordingly. We encourage you to take full advantage of this capability while it remains available at no extra cost.
